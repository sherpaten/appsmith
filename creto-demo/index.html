ABOUT THIS PROJECT

What is Creto operations?

Creto Operations is an internal business workspace concept that brings operational information into one place. Teams can review orders, monitor key metrics, search records and move routine work through clear action flows. It is intended to reduce spreadsheet-heavy processes and give staff a more consistent view of daily operations.

Built for: Operations teams, service businesses, support teams and organisations replacing manual internal workflows.
